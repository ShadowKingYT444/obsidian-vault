# Pericles

> A case in democratic leadership, alliance extraction, maritime strategy, and how a rational plan can be disrupted by disease and political succession.

## Operating model

- **Objective:** Preserve Athenian power by avoiding Spartan strength on land while using naval and financial superiority.
- **Constraints:** Democratic accountability, allied resentment, Spartan land power, and dependence on concentrated urban population.

- Linked domestic democratic legitimacy to imperial resources and public works.
- Used sea power to offset weaker land position.
- Argued for strategic patience rather than emotional pursuit of decisive land battle.

## Decisions to study

1. Moving rural Athenians behind the walls reduced exposure to Spartan raids.
2. Refusing major land battle fit Athens' comparative advantage.
3. Reliance on a dense city became catastrophic when plague struck.

## Failure modes and costs

- The strategy imposed severe social costs on evacuated citizens.
- His death removed a coordinating political figure and Athens later made more aggressive, inconsistent choices.

## Context you cannot copy

Athenian democracy, tribute from the Delian League, and naval infrastructure were preconditions for his strategy.
