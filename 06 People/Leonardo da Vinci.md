# Leonardo da Vinci

> A case in cross-domain observation and visual thinking, but also in the gap between abundant ideas and completed, transmitted work.

## Operating model

- **Objective:** Understand and represent natural and engineered systems through observation, drawing, and design.
- **Constraints:** Patronage dependence, limited mathematics in some domains, unavailable materials, and a tendency toward unfinished projects.

- Used drawing as an analytical instrument, not only an artistic output.
- Moved between anatomy, hydraulics, mechanics, optics, and painting to transfer visual models.
- Insisted on close observation when inherited authority conflicted with visible structure.

## Decisions to study

1. Human dissections produced unusually detailed anatomical studies.
2. Engineering notebooks explored mechanisms beyond immediate commission needs.
3. He repeatedly revised works such as the Mona Lisa, showing both refinement and completion cost.

## Failure modes and costs

- Many engineering designs were not built or tested, so feasibility is often overstated by modern admirers.
- His private notebooks limited immediate diffusion of some insights.

## Context you cannot copy

Renaissance workshops already combined art, craft, geometry, patronage, and engineering.
