# Charles Darwin

> A case in mechanism-first explanation: variation, inheritance, differential reproductive success, and deep time could generate complex adaptation without a top-down designer of each trait.

## Operating model

- **Objective:** Explain the patterned diversity and adaptation of living organisms through a natural causal process.
- **Constraints:** No genetics, incomplete fossil record, social/religious controversy, and difficulty observing macroevolution directly.

- Accumulated observations across biogeography, breeding, morphology, and paleontology.
- Used artificial selection as an analogy for how cumulative selection can reshape populations.
- Focused on a generative mechanism able to explain many classes of facts simultaneously.

## Decisions to study

1. Delayed publication while collecting evidence and refining arguments.
2. Wallace's independent formulation accelerated joint presentation in 1858.
3. On the Origin of Species foregrounded natural selection and extensive evidence while avoiding a full human-origins treatment until later.

## Failure modes and costs

- Darwin lacked Mendelian genetics and proposed incorrect ideas about heredity.
- Some later social and political ideologies misused evolutionary language far beyond Darwin's biological mechanism.
