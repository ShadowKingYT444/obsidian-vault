# Ibn Sina

> A model of synthesis: he integrated Greek philosophy, Islamic intellectual traditions, medicine, and original argument into systems that became teachable across centuries.

## Operating model

- **Objective:** Build coherent accounts of medicine, logic, metaphysics, psychology, and natural philosophy.
- **Constraints:** Political instability, court service, inherited Greek frameworks, and limited experimental medicine.

- Organized dispersed medical knowledge into a systematic diagnostic and therapeutic reference.
- Used rigorous conceptual distinctions in metaphysics, including essence and existence.
- Worked across disciplines rather than treating medicine and philosophy as isolated domains.

## Decisions to study

1. The Canon of Medicine prioritized structured, teachable organization over scattered case knowledge.
2. The Book of Healing built an encyclopedic philosophical system despite the title sounding medical.
3. Court service provided resources and access but repeatedly exposed him to political risk.

## Failure modes and costs

- Some medical and natural-philosophical claims reflected the limits of medieval evidence.
- Later reverence could turn his synthesis into authority rather than a starting point for correction.

## Context you cannot copy

Translation movements had already transmitted Aristotle, Galen, and other Greek traditions into Arabic intellectual life.
