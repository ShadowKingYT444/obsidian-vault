# Isaac Newton

> A model of deep unification: apparently different terrestrial and celestial phenomena became instances of a small mathematical system.

## Operating model

- **Objective:** Find mathematical laws that connect motion, force, optics, and astronomical observation.
- **Constraints:** Sparse data by modern standards, priority disputes, difficult notation, and limited measurement precision.

- Unified falling bodies and planetary motion through laws of motion and universal gravitation.
- Developed calculus methods to represent changing quantities.
- Moved between experiment, geometry, algebraic calculation, and astronomical data.

## Decisions to study

1. The Principia prioritized geometric proof to establish a rigorous mechanics.
2. Optical experiments treated color as a property of light composition rather than corruption by media.
3. As Master of the Mint, Newton also applied investigative intensity to currency enforcement.

## Failure modes and costs

- He invested enormous effort in alchemy and theological chronology that did not yield comparable scientific results.
- Priority conflicts, especially with Leibniz and Hooke, show the interpersonal cost of extreme defensiveness.

## Context you cannot copy

Kepler's laws, Galileo's mechanics, Descartes, Huygens, and a growing scientific network supplied prerequisites.
