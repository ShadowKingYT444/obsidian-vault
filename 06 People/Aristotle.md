# Aristotle

> A model of systematic decomposition: define categories, collect cases, distinguish causes, and build frameworks broad enough to organize many domains.

## Operating model

- **Objective:** Construct explanatory accounts of logic, nature, ethics, politics, rhetoric, and knowledge.
- **Constraints:** Limited instrumentation, mixed-quality empirical reports, and premodern physics.

- Separated different kinds of explanation rather than forcing one cause type.
- Used classification and comparative observation to organize complex domains.
- Connected ethics to repeated practice and politics to institutional forms.

## Decisions to study

1. Built a school that collected constitutions and natural observations.
2. Treated logic as an explicit technology of valid inference.
3. Developed frameworks that remained influential because they were modular and teachable.

## Failure modes and costs

- Some physical and biological claims were wrong and later authority sometimes protected them from challenge.
- Broad categories can become rigid if treated as final rather than provisional models.

## Context you cannot copy

His work grew from Greek philosophical debate and court/school networks, not solitary universal genius.
