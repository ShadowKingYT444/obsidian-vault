# Claude Shannon

> A model of finding the right abstraction: by separating information from meaning, Shannon made communication limits mathematically analyzable.

## Operating model

- **Objective:** Quantify information, noise, coding, and channel limits in a form independent of any specific message content.
- **Constraints:** Analog communications practice, noisy channels, hardware limits, and no mature digital communications theory.

- Represented uncertainty with entropy.
- Separated source coding from channel coding conceptually.
- Proved that reliable communication is possible below capacity even with noise, shifting engineering from heuristic to limit-based design.

## Decisions to study

1. His master's thesis connected Boolean algebra to switching circuits.
2. The 1948 paper deliberately abstracted away semantics to isolate the engineering problem.
3. He often built playful devices, showing a research style that mixed formal theory with physical tinkering.

## Failure modes and costs

- Information-theory vocabulary is often overextended into psychology, biology, or culture without matching the formal assumptions.
- Shannon's clean abstractions do not remove implementation complexity.

## Context you cannot copy

Bell Labs supplied communications problems, engineering talent, and institutional time for theory.
