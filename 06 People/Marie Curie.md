# Marie Curie

> A case in painstaking measurement and material separation: major discovery came from building a reliable signal out of noisy ores, repeated processing, and careful instrumentation.

## Operating model

- **Objective:** Understand unusual radiation from uranium compounds and identify the physical source of stronger-than-expected activity.
- **Constraints:** Crude equipment, enormous processing demands, uncertain atomic theory, gender barriers, and radiation hazards not yet understood.

- Used quantitative electrometer measurements to compare radiation across materials.
- Inferred new elements from activity that could not be explained by known substances.
- Processed large quantities of pitchblende to isolate evidence for polonium and radium.

## Decisions to study

1. Treating radioactivity as an atomic property helped shift thinking about supposedly indivisible atoms.
2. Sharing Nobel recognition with Pierre Curie and Becquerel came after institutional resistance to excluding her.
3. During World War I she helped deploy mobile radiography, translating physics into medical infrastructure.

## Failure modes and costs

- Long-term radiation exposure severely damaged health because risks were not understood.
- Hero stories can understate Pierre Curie, laboratory assistants, instrument makers, and the industrial labor behind ore processing.

## Context you cannot copy

Becquerel's uranium radiation discovery and Pierre Curie's electrometer work were direct prerequisites.
