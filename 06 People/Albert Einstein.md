# Albert Einstein

> A case in attacking contradictions between successful theories: Einstein repeatedly used simple thought experiments to expose where existing concepts of time, simultaneity, light, and gravity could not all remain intact.

## Operating model

- **Objective:** Build physical principles consistent across mechanics, electromagnetism, thermodynamics, and later gravitation.
- **Constraints:** Limited direct experimental access to extreme regimes and a physics community split across incompatible frameworks.

- Used invariance principles to restrict possible theories.
- Ran thought experiments that made hidden assumptions about observers and measurements explicit.
- Sought compact theoretical structures that explained multiple anomalies at once.

## Decisions to study

1. The 1905 papers addressed Brownian motion, photoelectricity, special relativity, and mass-energy equivalence through distinct but foundational arguments.
2. General relativity reinterpreted gravity geometrically rather than as an ordinary force.
3. His later skepticism about quantum indeterminacy motivated productive criticism but did not overturn quantum mechanics.

## Failure modes and costs

- Unified-field work consumed decades without comparable success.
- Popular lone-genius narratives understate Lorentz, Poincare, Minkowski, Grossmann, experimentalists, and the wider physics community.

## Context you cannot copy

Maxwellian electromagnetism, Lorentz transformations, precision experiments, statistical mechanics, and non-Euclidean geometry created the problem space.
