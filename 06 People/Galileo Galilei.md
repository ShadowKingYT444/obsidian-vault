# Galileo Galilei

> A case in combining instruments, mathematics, experiment, and argument to challenge inherited models—while showing that evidence disputes also occur inside institutions and politics.

## Operating model

- **Objective:** Use mathematical and observational evidence to explain motion and the heavens.
- **Constraints:** Imperfect telescopes, limited mechanics, Church censorship, and unsettled evidence for heliocentrism.

- Improved and exploited telescopic observation to reveal phenomena hard to reconcile with simple Aristotelian-Ptolemaic pictures.
- Used idealized experiments and mathematical relations to study motion.
- Communicated through dialogue and polemic, increasing both reach and institutional conflict.

## Decisions to study

1. Published telescopic observations rapidly in Sidereus Nuncius.
2. Advocated Copernicanism before all later physical evidence was available.
3. The 1632 Dialogue made the cosmological dispute more politically explosive.

## Failure modes and costs

- He could be combative and sometimes presented uncertain claims too strongly.
- The popular myth of a simple 'science versus religion' conflict hides legal, personal, evidential, and institutional complexity.

## Context you cannot copy

Copernicus, Tycho, Kepler, instrument makers, and court patronage were all part of the scientific transformation.
