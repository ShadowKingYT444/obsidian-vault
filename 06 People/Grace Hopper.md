# Grace Hopper

> A case in raising the abstraction layer: Hopper pushed programming away from machine-specific operations toward reusable languages and compilers that more people could use.

## Operating model

- **Objective:** Make computers programmable through higher-level, standardized languages rather than requiring every task to be expressed near machine code.
- **Constraints:** Slow hardware, skepticism about compilers, incompatible machines, and military/corporate standardization problems.

- Advocated automatic programming to translate higher-level instructions into machine operations.
- Worked on FLOW-MATIC and influenced COBOL standardization.
- Used vivid teaching examples and organizational persuasion to make abstract software ideas legible to managers and programmers.

## Decisions to study

1. Pursuing compilers challenged the belief that computers could only do arithmetic-oriented coding directly.
2. COBOL's English-like business syntax favored portability and accessibility over elegance.
3. Long Navy service linked technical development to large institutional deployment.

## Failure modes and costs

- Popular quotes and anecdotes attributed to Hopper are sometimes weakly sourced.
- COBOL solved real portability/business problems but later accumulated legacy-system costs.

## Context you cannot copy

Hopper worked within teams at Harvard, Eckert-Mauchly/Remington Rand, standards committees, and the US Navy.
