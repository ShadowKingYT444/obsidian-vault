# Florence Nightingale

> A case in converting observation and statistics into administrative reform: measurement mattered because she connected it to sanitation, reporting, and institutional redesign.

## Operating model

- **Objective:** Reduce preventable mortality and professionalize nursing and hospital administration.
- **Constraints:** Weak hospital data, military bureaucracy, gender barriers, contested causal theories of disease, and institutional resistance.

- Collected and visualized mortality data to make administrative failure legible.
- Focused on sanitation, ventilation, supplies, and process rather than heroic bedside intervention alone.
- Built training and reporting systems that persisted beyond one crisis.

## Decisions to study

1. Work at Scutari became a platform for wider military-health reform.
2. Her polar-area diagrams helped communicate causes of mortality to non-specialists.
3. Later work on Indian sanitation showed a shift from local nursing identity to systems administration.

## Failure modes and costs

- Some popular accounts overstate the immediate mortality effect of her earliest Crimean interventions; sanitation improvements involved multiple actors and timing.
- She could be rigid and controlling in organizational relationships.

## Context you cannot copy

The sanitary movement, statistical societies, army reformers, and emerging public-health institutions supplied networks and concepts.
