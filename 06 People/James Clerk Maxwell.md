# James Clerk Maxwell

> A model of theoretical compression: Maxwell turned scattered electrical, magnetic, and optical phenomena into a field theory whose equations implied new facts such as electromagnetic waves.

## Operating model

- **Objective:** Create a coherent mathematical representation of electricity and magnetism consistent with known experiments.
- **Constraints:** Competing mechanical analogies, incomplete measurements, complex notation, and a fragmented experimental literature.

- Translated Faraday's field intuition into mathematical relations.
- Added the displacement-current term needed for charge continuity and a consistent field theory.
- Recognized that predicted electromagnetic-wave speed was close to measured light speed, linking optics and electromagnetism.

## Decisions to study

1. Used physical analogies as temporary reasoning tools while extracting equations that survived the analogies.
2. A Treatise on Electricity and Magnetism synthesized a large field rather than one experiment.
3. Statistical work in kinetic theory showed similar cross-domain mathematical transfer.

## Failure modes and costs

- His original notation was difficult; later Heaviside/Gibbs vector reformulations made the theory easier to use.
- Theoretical elegance still depended on experimental confirmation by later researchers such as Hertz.

Faraday's experiments, precision electrical measurement, and nineteenth-century mathematical physics were prerequisites.
