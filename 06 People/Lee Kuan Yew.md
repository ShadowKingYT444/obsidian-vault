# Lee Kuan Yew

> A compact case in building credible administration, housing, infrastructure, education, and investment policy under extreme geographic vulnerability—paired with constrained political competition.

## Operating model

- **Objective:** Make a small city-state economically viable, secure, governable, and attractive to trade and investment.
- **Constraints:** No large domestic market, communal tension, unemployment, limited land, geopolitical exposure, and separation from Malaysia.

- Built a highly capable civil service with strong anti-corruption enforcement.
- Used public housing and urban planning as both social policy and state-building infrastructure.
- Welcomed foreign capital while investing in ports, education, and workforce capability.

## Decisions to study

1. Maintaining openness to multinational firms avoided dependence on a protected domestic industrial base.
2. Housing policy created broad asset ownership but also deep state influence over land and urban life.
3. Strict controls on media, assembly, and opposition increased governability at the cost of political freedom.

## Failure modes and costs

- Singapore's scale, location, and city-state structure make direct copying difficult.
- High administrative competence does not erase civil-liberty tradeoffs or distributional tensions.

## Context you cannot copy

Singapore inherited a major port, British legal/administrative legacies, a strategic location, and access to postwar global trade.
