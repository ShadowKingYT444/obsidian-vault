# Archimedes

> A case in moving between ideal mathematical models and physical mechanisms while keeping proof and engineering intuition distinct.

## Operating model

- **Objective:** Solve geometric and mechanical problems with rigorous argument and leverage mathematical invariants.
- **Constraints:** No modern algebraic notation, limited experimental instruments, and fragmentary surviving works.

- Reduced hard geometric problems to ratios, exhaustion arguments, and balance concepts.
- Used idealized mechanics to reason about centers of gravity and levers.
- Combined theoretical prestige with practical engineering traditions.

## Decisions to study

1. Developed exhaustion methods that anticipated later integral reasoning.
2. Used hydrostatic reasoning to connect displacement and buoyancy.
3. Syracusan defensive machines became part of later legend, though many details are hard to verify.

## Failure modes and costs

- Popular stories often blur documented mathematics with later heroic engineering anecdotes.
- His notation and transmission context limited direct continuity into later mathematics.

## Context you cannot copy

Hellenistic mathematics already had strong geometric proof traditions through Euclid and others.
