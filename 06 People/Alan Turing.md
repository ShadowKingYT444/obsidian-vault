# Alan Turing

> A case in defining the object before building it: Turing clarified what computation means abstractly, then worked on concrete cryptanalytic and machine problems.

## Operating model

- **Objective:** Understand the limits of mechanical computation and apply formal reasoning to codebreaking and machine intelligence.
- **Constraints:** Pre-electronic computing, wartime secrecy, limited hardware, and later criminal persecution for homosexuality.

- Reduced computation to a minimal abstract machine, separating logical capability from a specific device.
- Used statistical and logical structure to reduce cryptanalytic search.
- Framed machine intelligence through operational tests rather than requiring a definition of consciousness.

## Decisions to study

1. The 1936 computability paper created a durable formal model.
2. At Bletchley Park, Turing contributed methods and machine concepts for attacking Enigma traffic within a much larger team.
3. The 1950 imitation-game paper shifted debate from metaphysical labels toward observable interaction.

## Failure modes and costs

- Popular media often exaggerate a single-person 'Turing cracked Enigma' story.
- British prosecution and forced hormonal treatment were institutional harms that shortened and damaged his life.

## Context you cannot copy

Polish cryptanalysts, Bletchley teams, electromechanical engineering, and wartime intelligence systems were essential.
