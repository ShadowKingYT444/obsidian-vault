# Franklin D Roosevelt

> A case in governing under uncertainty through experimentation, coalition building, communication, and institutional expansion rather than one fixed master plan.

## Operating model

- **Objective:** Restore political and economic stability during the Depression, then mobilize the United States for global war.
- **Constraints:** Mass unemployment, banking panic, constitutional limits, regional party factions, isolationism, and later a two-ocean war.

- Used rapid policy experimentation and accepted that some programs would fail.
- Built a broad electoral coalition across regions, labor, urban machines, farmers, and many minority voters.
- Used radio and political communication to reduce uncertainty and explain state action.

## Decisions to study

1. The 1933 bank holiday and banking measures targeted panic and confidence quickly.
2. The New Deal expanded federal administrative capacity but did not by itself end all Depression unemployment.
3. Lend-Lease and prewar mobilization increased support for Britain before formal US entry.

## Failure modes and costs

- The 1937 court-packing plan damaged political capital.
- Japanese American incarceration under Executive Order 9066 was a major civil-liberties injustice.
- New Deal compromises often preserved racial exclusion to retain southern Democrats.

## Context you cannot copy

Federal institutions, industrial depth, dollar finance, and wartime demand mattered alongside Roosevelt's leadership.
