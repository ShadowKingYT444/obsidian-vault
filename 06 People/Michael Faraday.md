# Michael Faraday

> A model of experimental intuition: Faraday built reliable qualitative structures around fields and induction before the mathematics needed to compress them was fully available.

## Operating model

- **Objective:** Discover reproducible relations among electricity, magnetism, chemistry, and matter.
- **Constraints:** Limited formal mathematics, primitive instruments, and rapidly changing experimental knowledge.

- Designed simple experiments that isolated one physical relation at a time.
- Used lines of force as a spatial model for electromagnetic interaction.
- Kept detailed laboratory notebooks and repeated demonstrations to test stability.

## Decisions to study

1. The 1831 induction experiments linked changing magnetic conditions to electric current.
2. The electric motor/generator principle opened a path from phenomenon to machines.
3. Public lectures and demonstrations made experimental reasoning visible beyond specialist circles.

## Failure modes and costs

- Qualitative field concepts needed later mathematical formalization for broader predictive power.
- Biographical stories about humble genius can obscure the Royal Institution, Davy, instrument makers, and scientific networks.

## Context you cannot copy

Faraday worked inside a well-equipped institution and inherited Volta, Oersted, Ampere, Davy, and others' discoveries.
