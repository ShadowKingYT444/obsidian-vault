# Augustus

> A master case in making concentrated power durable by embedding it inside familiar institutions, incentives, and narratives.

## Operating model

- **Objective:** End recurrent civil war while preserving enough republican legitimacy to make one-man supremacy acceptable.
- **Constraints:** Recent assassinations, exhausted elites, veteran demands, and Roman hostility to kingship.

- Accumulated powers gradually while preserving republican offices and language.
- Controlled military appointments and veteran settlement.
- Used building, ritual, family policy, literature, and peace narratives to normalize the new order.

## Decisions to study

1. Triumviral violence helped eliminate rivals but created a legitimacy debt.
2. Defeating Antony and Cleopatra removed the last peer competitor.
3. The constitutional settlements of 27 and 23 BCE distributed titles and powers to make monarchy less explicit.

## Failure modes and costs

- Succession remained difficult and dependent on family/adoption politics.
- The system reduced elite competition by narrowing meaningful political autonomy.

## Context you cannot copy

Augustus benefited from war exhaustion and from Caesar's destruction of the old equilibrium.
