# Logistics Before Tactics

> A plan is impossible if people, energy, food, ammunition, money, or information cannot arrive at the required place and time.

## Mechanism

- Logistics sets the feasible tempo and radius of action.
- Supply failure compounds through fatigue, maintenance, and delay.
- Operational brilliance cannot permanently repeal physical constraints.

## Cases

- Hannibal repeatedly won battles in Italy but could not convert them into Roman political collapse.
- Napoleon's 1812 campaign exceeded the supply and replacement system that supported earlier rapid campaigns.
- Industrial firms hit the same class of bottleneck when demand grows faster than yield or supplier capacity.

## Boundary conditions / failure cases

- Superior logistics does not guarantee tactical competence.
- Living off the land increases speed only where density, season, and politics allow it.

## Retrieval and transfer questions

- Which resource is the true bottleneck?
- What happens when throughput falls by 30 percent?
