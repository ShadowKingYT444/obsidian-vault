# General-Purpose Technologies and Complementary Systems

> Transformative technologies create large gains only after infrastructure, skills, standards, organizations, and business models adapt.

## Mechanism

- A new capability initially enters an old system designed for older constraints.
- Complementary investments unlock second-order uses.
- Diffusion often matters more economically than the first prototype.

## Cases

- Printing required paper, literacy, distribution, and publishing institutions.
- Electricity produced large factory gains after layouts and motors were redesigned rather than merely replacing steam shafts.
- The internet became central after browsers, networks, payments, cloud infrastructure, and business models matured.

## Boundary conditions / failure cases

- Not every invention is a general-purpose technology.
- Complementary change can take decades and create productivity lags.

## Retrieval and transfer questions

- What old system limits the new technology?
- Which complement is scarce?
