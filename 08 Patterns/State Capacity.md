# State Capacity

> A state's formal ambition matters less than its ability to measure, tax, communicate, enforce, and mobilize.

## Mechanism

- Reliable revenue funds armies, infrastructure, administration, and crisis response.
- Records and measurement let central policy reach local reality.
- Legitimate enforcement lowers the cost of compliance.

## Cases

- Qin standardization increased administrative reach across conquered territories.
- Early modern warfare rewarded states that could borrow and tax at scale.
- Meiji Japan paired institutional reform with conscription, education, and industrial policy.

## Boundary conditions / failure cases

- High capacity can support repression as easily as public goods.
- Overcentralization can destroy local information and adaptation.

## Retrieval and transfer questions

- What can the center actually observe?
- How quickly can resources move from decision to execution?
