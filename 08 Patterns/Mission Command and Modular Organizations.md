# Mission Command and Modular Organizations

> Semi-autonomous units reduce central coordination load when each unit has enough capability and a clear shared objective.

## Mechanism

- Local leaders can act on information the center does not have.
- Modularity contains failures and lets units move in parallel.
- Shared intent substitutes for detailed instruction when communication is slow.

## Cases

- Napoleonic corps combined multiple arms and could operate for limited periods independently.
- Mongol decimal organization supported delegation and reconfiguration.
- Modern product teams can own a service end to end when interfaces are explicit.

## Boundary conditions / failure cases

- Autonomy fails when objectives conflict or interfaces are ambiguous.
- Highly coupled systems may require tighter central coordination.
- A weak selection system creates autonomous incompetence.

## Retrieval and transfer questions

- What decision can safely move to the edge?
- What interfaces must remain standardized?
