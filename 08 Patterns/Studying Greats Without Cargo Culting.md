# Studying Greats Without Cargo Culting

> Biography is useful when it produces testable mechanisms, not imitation of surface behavior.

## Mechanism

- Great lives compress many high-stakes decisions into one narrative.
- Comparing success and failure inside the same life controls for some personality differences.
- Cross-case comparison can reveal mechanisms that survive changes in era and domain.

## Cases

- Napoleon studied earlier commanders, but useful transfer required adapting principles to new institutions and technology.
- Scientists such as Newton and Einstein built on predecessors by changing models, not by copying schedules.
- Industrial leaders often copied systems such as standardization or vertical integration while changing implementation.

## Boundary conditions / failure cases

- Anecdotes are not causal proof.
- Extreme work hours can be a symptom of crisis or poor system design, not a universal advantage.
- Famous survivors are selected from a larger population of equally intense people who failed.

## Retrieval and transfer questions

- What mechanism sits between the anecdote and the claimed lesson?
- Where did the same trait hurt the person?
- Would the lesson survive if the famous name were removed?
