# Information Compression and Decision Latency

> Large organizations need representations that compress reality without deleting the variables that drive decisions.

## Mechanism

- Leaders cannot inspect every local detail.
- Standard reports, metrics, maps, and abstractions reduce bandwidth.
- Decision quality depends on whether compression preserves causal signal.

## Cases

- Military staffs convert dispersed reports into operational pictures.
- Scientific notation and models compress observations into manipulable structure.
- Dashboards help firms unless metrics become targets and detach from reality.

## Boundary conditions / failure cases

- Compression creates blind spots.
- Too many metrics recreate the raw-data problem.
- Fast communication of bad summaries accelerates wrong decisions.

## Retrieval and transfer questions

- Which variable was discarded by the summary?
- Can a local anomaly bypass the reporting hierarchy?
