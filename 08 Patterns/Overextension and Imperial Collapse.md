# Overextension and Imperial Collapse

> Expansion can increase power while increasing frontier, communication, defense, and governance costs faster than revenue.

## Mechanism

- Distance increases latency and transport cost.
- New territories create new rivals and commitments.
- Success encourages leaders to treat favorable conditions as permanent.

## Cases

- Napoleon's Iberian and Russian commitments stretched French military and political capacity.
- Large empires often relied on local intermediaries because direct control was expensive.
- Firms can add products faster than operations and management systems scale.

## Boundary conditions / failure cases

- Large scale can create tax, market, and defense economies.
- Institutional modularity and cheap transport can shift the threshold.

## Retrieval and transfer questions

- Which marginal expansion adds more obligation than usable capacity?
- What commitment can be abandoned without collapsing credibility?
