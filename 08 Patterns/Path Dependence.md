# Path Dependence

> Early choices can persist because later systems, skills, expectations, and switching costs build around them.

## Mechanism

- Coordination rewards compatibility with installed standards.
- Institutions create beneficiaries who defend arrangements.
- Training and infrastructure make alternatives costly even when technically better.

## Cases

- Political constitutions preserve compromises long after original conditions change.
- Technical standards survive because ecosystems form around them.
- Industrial geography can persist after the original resource advantage weakens.

## Boundary conditions / failure cases

- Path dependence is not destiny; shocks or large gains can break lock-in.
- Do not use it as a vague explanation for every persistence.

## Retrieval and transfer questions

- What increasing return preserves the path?
- What size shock would make switching rational?
