# Meritocracy, Selection, and Elite Formation

> Performance improves when institutions identify capable people and give them responsibility, but every merit system defines and can game its own metric.

## Mechanism

- Open promotion widens the talent pool.
- High-feedback environments reveal competence faster.
- Elite circulation can reduce hereditary stagnation.

## Cases

- Revolutionary and Napoleonic France opened some advancement beyond old aristocratic channels while preserving patronage and politics.
- Imperial Chinese examinations broadened bureaucratic selection but optimized for specific literati skills.
- Technology firms can promote strong specialists while still mismeasuring management ability.

## Boundary conditions / failure cases

- No system measures pure merit.
- Selection can favor conformity, test skill, network access, or willingness to overwork.
- A meritocratic story can hide unequal opportunity.

## Retrieval and transfer questions

- What is being measured?
- Which valuable people does the filter systematically miss?
