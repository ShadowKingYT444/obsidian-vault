# Legitimacy and Narrative

> Power becomes cheaper to maintain when people believe authority is rightful, useful, inevitable, or identity-consistent.

## Mechanism

- Legitimacy reduces monitoring and coercion costs.
- Narratives coordinate expectations about authority.
- Symbols and ceremonies can turn abstract institutions into shared identity.

## Cases

- Augustus preserved republican forms while concentrating imperial power.
- Revolutionary regimes claim new sources of legitimacy when dynastic claims collapse.
- Companies use mission narratives to coordinate effort, but rhetoric fails when incentives contradict it.

## Boundary conditions / failure cases

- Narrative cannot indefinitely compensate for material failure.
- Different groups can hold incompatible legitimacy models.
- Propaganda is not identical to legitimacy.

## Retrieval and transfer questions

- Why do followers think obedience is justified?
- Which group rejects the dominant narrative, and why?
