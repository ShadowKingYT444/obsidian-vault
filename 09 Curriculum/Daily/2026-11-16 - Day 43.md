# Day 43 — Darwin — mechanism before conclusion

**Target time:** 35 minutes

## Read in this order

1. [[Charles Darwin]]
2. [[Darwinian Revolution in Biology]]
3. [[Compounding and Learning Curves]]

## Before you read

> Why is natural selection stronger as an explanation than simply saying species change over time?

Make a prediction before opening the notes.

## After you read

Without reopening the notes, write:

1. the core causal mechanism;
2. the strongest decision;
3. one failure mode;
4. one fact that changed your prior model.

## Transfer

> For one problem, replace a descriptive label with a mechanism that could generate the observations.

## Stop condition

Stop when you can reconstruct and transfer the mechanism. Do not read for completion theater.
