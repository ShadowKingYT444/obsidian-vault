# Day 18 — Tokugawa Ieyasu — turn victory into a stable system

**Target time:** 30 minutes

## Read in this order

1. [[Tokugawa Ieyasu]]
2. [[Tokugawa Settlement of Japan]]
3. [[Path Dependence]]

## Before you read

Answer from your current model:

> What made renewed civil war progressively more expensive under Tokugawa institutions?

Do not look for the answer first. Make a prediction.

## After you read

Without reopening the notes, write:

1. the causal mechanism in 2–3 sentences;
2. one decision that looked good at the time;
3. one failure mode or boundary condition;
4. one fact that changed your prior model.

## Transfer

> After your next win, what process makes the result persist without repeated heroic effort?

## Stop condition

Stop when you can reconstruct the mechanism and transfer it. Do not keep reading for completion theater.
