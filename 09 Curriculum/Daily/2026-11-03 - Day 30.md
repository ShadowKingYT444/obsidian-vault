# Day 30 — Faraday — experimental intuition

**Target time:** 30 minutes

## Read in this order

1. [[Michael Faraday]]
2. [[Electrification and the Second Industrial Revolution]]
3. [[Compounding and Learning Curves]]

## Before you read

> How did Faraday produce deep insight despite limited formal mathematics?

Make a prediction before opening the notes.

## After you read

Without reopening the notes, write:

1. the core causal mechanism;
2. the strongest decision;
3. one failure mode;
4. one fact that changed your prior model.

## Transfer

> Design one cheap experiment that isolates a variable you currently only speculate about.

## Stop condition

Stop when you can reconstruct and transfer the mechanism. Do not read for completion theater.
