# Day 84 — Final reconstruction — no notes first

**Target time:** 60 minutes

## Read in this order

1. [[History Retrieval Queue]]
2. [[Polymath Dashboard]]

## Before you read

> Without opening any biography, can you reconstruct twelve people, twelve events, and eight mechanisms with causal links between them?

Make a prediction before opening the notes.

## After you read

Without reopening the notes, write:

1. the core causal mechanism;
2. the strongest decision;
3. one failure mode;
4. one fact that changed your prior model.

## Transfer

> Write a one-page memo: 'My operating model after 12 weeks of studying history.' Then choose the weakest three mechanisms for Cycle 2.

## Stop condition

Stop when you can reconstruct and transfer the mechanism. Do not read for completion theater.
