# Day 32 — Edison versus Tesla

**Target time:** 40 minutes

## Read in this order

1. [[Thomas Edison]]
2. [[Nikola Tesla]]
3. [[War of Currents]]

## Before you read

> Why is 'Tesla was the genius and Edison was the businessman' a weak model of the AC/DC transition?

Make a prediction before opening the notes.

## After you read

Without reopening the notes, write:

1. the core causal mechanism;
2. the strongest decision;
3. one failure mode;
4. one fact that changed your prior model.

## Transfer

> Take one idea you like and list the financing, manufacturing, standards, distribution, and user complements required to make it real.

## Stop condition

Stop when you can reconstruct and transfer the mechanism. Do not read for completion theater.
