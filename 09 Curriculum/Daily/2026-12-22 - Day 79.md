# Day 79 — Aristotle — classify before you generalize

**Target time:** 35 minutes

## Read in this order

1. [[Aristotle]]
2. [[Information Compression and Decision Latency]]
3. [[Path Dependence]]

## Before you read

> Why can a classification system remain useful even after many individual claims inside it become false?

Make a prediction before opening the notes.

## After you read

Without reopening the notes, write:

1. the core causal mechanism;
2. the strongest decision;
3. one failure mode;
4. one fact that changed your prior model.

## Transfer

> Build a taxonomy for one messy domain, then name one edge case that breaks it.

## Stop condition

Stop when you can reconstruct and transfer the mechanism. Do not read for completion theater.
