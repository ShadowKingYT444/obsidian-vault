# Day 19 — Galileo — instruments change what can be known

**Target time:** 35 minutes

## Read in this order

1. [[Galileo Galilei]]
2. [[Scientific Revolution]]
3. [[General-Purpose Technologies and Complementary Systems]]

## Before you read

Answer from your current model:

> Which Galilean observations directly challenged older models, and which only shifted plausibility?

Do not look for the answer first. Make a prediction.

## After you read

Without reopening the notes, write:

1. the causal mechanism in 2–3 sentences;
2. one decision that looked good at the time;
3. one failure mode or boundary condition;
4. one fact that changed your prior model.

## Transfer

> What new instrument, benchmark, or measurement would let you see something you currently argue about abstractly?

## Stop condition

Stop when you can reconstruct the mechanism and transfer it. Do not keep reading for completion theater.
