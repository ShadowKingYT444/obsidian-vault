# Day 45 — Einstein — attack contradictions

**Target time:** 35 minutes

## Read in this order

1. [[Albert Einstein]]
2. [[Relativity and Quantum Revolution]]
3. [[Studying Greats Without Cargo Culting]]

## Before you read

> Which contradiction does special relativity resolve rather than merely redescribe?

Make a prediction before opening the notes.

## After you read

Without reopening the notes, write:

1. the core causal mechanism;
2. the strongest decision;
3. one failure mode;
4. one fact that changed your prior model.

## Transfer

> List two beliefs in your current model that create incompatible predictions.

## Stop condition

Stop when you can reconstruct and transfer the mechanism. Do not read for completion theater.
