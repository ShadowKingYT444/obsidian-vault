# Day 55 — Churchill — crisis communication with real constraints

**Target time:** 35 minutes

## Read in this order

1. [[Winston Churchill]]
2. [[World War II]]
3. [[Legitimacy and Narrative]]

## Before you read

> What did Churchill actually change in 1940, and what capabilities were supplied by Britain as a system?

Make a prediction before opening the notes.

## After you read

Without reopening the notes, write:

1. the core causal mechanism;
2. the strongest decision;
3. one failure mode;
4. one fact that changed your prior model.

## Transfer

> Write a mission statement that changes behavior because it names a real constraint and action, not because it sounds inspiring.

## Stop condition

Stop when you can reconstruct and transfer the mechanism. Do not read for completion theater.
