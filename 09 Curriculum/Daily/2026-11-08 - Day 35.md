# Day 35 — Meiji Japan — copy mechanisms, not costumes

**Target time:** 35 minutes

## Read in this order

1. [[Meiji Restoration]]
2. [[Studying Greats Without Cargo Culting]]
3. [[State Capacity]]

## Before you read

> Why did imported institutions work better in Japan than a simple 'copy the West' story predicts?

Make a prediction before opening the notes.

## After you read

Without reopening the notes, write:

1. the core causal mechanism;
2. the strongest decision;
3. one failure mode;
4. one fact that changed your prior model.

## Transfer

> Choose one practice from a high-performing person or team and state the prerequisite conditions before copying it.

## Stop condition

Stop when you can reconstruct and transfer the mechanism. Do not read for completion theater.
