## What needs skepticism

The transcript often moves too quickly from similarity to causation.

- Two people working long hours does not show that long hours caused their success.
- "Both think like physicists" is a loose analogy unless a specific decision process is defined.
- SpaceX functional teams are not literally Napoleonic corps; the useful comparison is modular autonomy and fast coordination.
- "Meritocracy" must be measured against actual promotion/selection behavior, not only leader rhetoric.
- Biography anecdotes are selected after success and have strong survivorship bias.
- Napoleon's system depended on Revolutionary France, mass mobilization, staff institutions, geography, and opponents. Musk's firms depend on capital markets, government procurement, modern engineering, software, regulation, and supply chains.
