# Learner Model

## Current goals

- 

## Stable foundations

Only concepts supported by meaningful retrieval/reasoning evidence.

- 

## Active frontier

Links to concepts currently being learned.

- 

## Fragile knowledge

Correct in some contexts but not yet robust to reconstruction or transfer.

- 

## Misconceptions

Record the learner's *wrong generative rule*, not merely a wrong answer.

- 

## Diagnostic patterns

Patterns that improve future question selection.

Examples:
- knows formulas but often lacks semantic interpretation;
- strong verbal intuition but weak matrix-shape bookkeeping;
- recognizes concepts but needs transfer testing;
- tends to overgeneralize rules beyond their assumptions.

## Teaching preferences

- First-principles derivation over arbitrary rules.
- Every important concept should have a visible motivation.
- One meaningful reasoning step at a time.
- Extreme depth on high-leverage generating ideas.
- Compress or skip material already understood deeply.
- Do not waste time on peripheral detail unless it changes the core model.
- Use difficult, discriminative questions rather than generic quiz volume.
- Challenge explanations and require transfer before calling the primary target secure.
